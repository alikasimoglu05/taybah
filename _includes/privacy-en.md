# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

- **Learning progress** (lessons, words, streak, settings, display name) is stored **only on your device**. Taybah has no accounts or own servers and does not receive this data.
- **Reminders** are scheduled locally on your device. **Audio:** Arabic words and phrases are pre-recorded with ElevenLabs and built into the app. If a recording is missing, your device's built-in speech synthesis is used. No data is sent during playback.
- **Purchases** are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details. Subscription status is verified by **RevenueCat**, which receives an anonymous customer ID, purchase details and basic device data. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Usage statistics:** to improve the app we collect anonymous usage events (for example, which onboarding screens were viewed, whether the subscription screen was opened, app opens) together with basic device data (model, OS and app version, language). They are linked only to a random anonymous ID, never to your name or contacts, and are processed by **PostHog** on servers in the EU. PostHog privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy
- No ads, no cross-app tracking, no selling or sharing of data beyond the above.
- Deleting the app removes all progress. Cancel subscriptions in App Store or Google Play settings.

# Taybah Privacy Policy (English)

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

- **Learning progress** (lessons, words, streak, settings, display name) is stored **only on your device**. Taybah has no accounts or own servers and does not receive this data.
- **Reminders** are scheduled locally on your device. **Audio** uses your device's built-in speech synthesis.
- **Purchases** are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details. Subscription status is verified by **RevenueCat**, which receives an anonymous customer ID, purchase details and basic device data. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- No ads, no cross-app tracking, no selling or sharing of data beyond the above.
- Deleting the app removes all progress. Cancel subscriptions in App Store or Google Play settings.

# Support

Have a question, found a bug or want to suggest an improvement? Email us: [alikasimoglu05@icloud.com](mailto:alikasimoglu05@icloud.com)

## Taybah Plus subscription
Subscriptions are started and cancelled in App Store or Google Play settings. To restore a purchase on a new device, open **Settings → Taybah Plus → Restore purchases** in the app.

## Progress
Your progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Delete your data
Taybah has no accounts, and your learning progress never leaves your device.

1. **Learning progress:** delete the Taybah app from your phone. All lessons, words, streaks and settings are removed immediately.
2. **Subscription records:** our subscription service (RevenueCat) keeps an anonymous customer ID and your purchase history. To have them deleted, email [alikasimoglu05@icloud.com](mailto:alikasimoglu05@icloud.com) with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) or App Store receipt. We delete these records within 30 days and confirm by email.

Payment records kept by Google or Apple are governed by their own policies. Deleting your data does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it in Google Play or App Store settings first.

## Taybah Plus subscription
Subscriptions are started and cancelled in App Store or Google Play settings. To restore a purchase on a new device, open **Settings → Taybah Plus → Restore purchases** in the app.

## Progress
Your progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
